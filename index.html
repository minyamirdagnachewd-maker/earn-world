🇪🇹 Ethiopia focus

Local context. Global ambition.

We aim to make online-work information easier to understand for people in Ethiopia, including practical notes about eligibility, language, internet access and payment methods.

What we will prioritize

Useful, realistic guidance

  • Opportunities open to applicants in Ethiopia
  • Clear payment and identity-verification requirements
  • Free courses and beginner-friendly skills
  • Amharic explanations alongside English

A simple starting plan

Turn learning into progress

Choose one useful skill

Pick something you can practice consistently, such as writing, translation or spreadsheets.

Create two or three sample projects

Show what you can do. Do not claim client experience you do not have.

Check opportunities carefully

Confirm country eligibility, age rules, fees, payment methods and the identity of the organization.

Start small and improve

Ask for feedback, keep records and never promise results you cannot deliver.

Online safety comes first. Never pay a fee to unlock a promised job, share passwords or one-time codes, or trust guaranteed-income claims. Check the official company website and ask a trusted adult for help before sharing personal or payment information. Some work platforms require users to be 18 or older.
